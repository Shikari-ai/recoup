The ceiling is low because the problem is genuinely noisy, not because
the model is weak. Recovery is a coin flip weighted by context: two
receivables with a 30% and a 40% chance will disagree with their
probabilities often enough to cap AUC near 0.78 for ANY model.

Note that OBSERVABLE-ONLY sits essentially on top of ORACLE. The latent
per-customer traits contribute almost nothing to rankability, so the
model is not starved of information -- it is bounded by noise. That is
why adding features to close the gap did not work, and why the
remaining gap is estimation error rather than a missing signal.
