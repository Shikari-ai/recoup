What the model learned

Coefficients on the logit scale, largest magnitude first. Every one is readable — which is why this is logistic regression and not a gradient-boosted ensemble.
These numbers come from a simulation. The absolute rupee figures are a property of that simulation. The claim being made is the comparison: every arm ran over identical held-out events, identical guardrails, identical action costs and identical random draws, and only the decision logic differed. Organic recoveries — payers who would have returned unaided — are credited equally to every arm and subtracted before lift is computed. See docs/EVALUATION.md and recoup/sim/world.py.
